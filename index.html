// THE PROBLEM

Your Security Stack Is Not Your Attack Surface.

A scanner tells you which of your tools have a known CVE. It has no idea that a low-severity bug in your login form, chained to an over-permissioned API key, chained to a forgotten staging subdomain, adds up to a full account takeover. Real attackers don't look for one weakness — they chain the ones your tools rated "informational."

Attackers don't care about your vulnerability report. They care about what they can chain together. That's what I test.
