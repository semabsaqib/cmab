▍ THE PROBLEM

Your Security Stack Is Not Your Attack Surface. Your Attack Surface Is What Chains Together.

Scanners find known weaknesses. Real attackers chain weaknesses together — a low-severity misconfiguration here, a forgotten subdomain there, a permission an AI agent was never meant to have — until one quiet path leads all the way to impact.

Attackers don't care about your vulnerability report. They care about what they can chain together. That's what I test — and it's why every engagement runs the same four-stage methodology, whether the target is a network, an application, or an autonomous agent.

// METHODOLOGY

Find. Prove. Explain. Fix.

The same four-stage process behind every engagement — the difference between a scanner output and a finding you can act on.

Prioritized remediation guidance, and a free retest once fixes ship, so the gap is closed, not just documented.
