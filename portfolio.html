// RaaS Infrastructure Compromise · Resecurity HUNTER

We Broke Into the Ransomware Crew Before They Could Break You.

Known Aliases BlackLock El Dorado Eldorado Mamona

As part of the Resecurity HUNTER effort against BlackLock — a ransomware-as-a-service operation on track to become the most prolific of 2025 — the team found a Local File Include flaw in the group's TOR leak site and turned it inward, exposing server config, credentials, and command history. Their fatal mistake: reused passwords, valid across multiple accounts.

"Defense that only counts victims is already losing. Get inside the adversary's process and turn it against them before the payload lands."

The team pulled 7 TB of stolen data and warned victims before it went public — 13 days ahead of one planned leak. By March, BlackLock and Mamona were offline and the operator had vanished.

// Operation Timeline
  • Vulnerability Discovery
    LFI flaw identified in BlackLock's TOR-hosted data-leak site during the winter holiday window.
  • Infrastructure Compromise
    Server-side config, credentials, and MEGA exfil accounts recovered; clearnet IPs behind the hidden service exposed.
  • Intelligence Collection
    Command history and victim publication schedule captured — 7 TB of compromised data catalogued and attributed.
  • Victim Interdiction
    Undisclosed victims alerted through national authorities days before their data was due to be leaked.
  • Disruption
    OPSEC failures exposed; affiliate trust collapsed; BlackLock and Mamona taken offline, operator gone dark.
